Ingólfr Arnarson is named in the Book of Settlements as the first permanent settler. He wintered at
Ingólfshöfði on the south coast and sent his slaves west along the shore to look for the high seat
pillars he had thrown overboard. They were found in a bay of smoke and steam, and the farm he built
there took its name from them: [Reykjavík](@reykjavik).
